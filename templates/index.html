How this works

Automated, not editorial

Listings are compiled by a program that reads public notices and posted agendas every day. No one decides what is newsworthy enough to include.

Sources are always linked

Every listing links to the notice it came from, so you can check the original rather than take our word for it.

Open data

The whole meeting dataset is public JSON. Reuse it, check it, or build on it.
